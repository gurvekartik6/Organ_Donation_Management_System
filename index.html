Organ Donation

What is Organ & Tissue Donation?

Organ and tissue donation is the process of donating organs or tissues from a living or deceased person to someone in need of a transplant. It helps save and improve lives by replacing damaged or non-functioning organs and tissues.

What is Transpantation?

Transplantation is a medical procedure in which a healthy organ or tissue from a donor is surgically placed into a recipient whose organ has failed or been damaged.

How can pledge to donate organs?

Pledging to donate organs is a voluntary decision that involves registering as an organ donor.
